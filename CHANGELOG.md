# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release: the skeleton.

- ASCII art skeleton (front view) with 19 bones or bone groups: skull, jaw,
  cervical and lumbar vertebrae, shoulder blade, collarbone, sternum, ribs,
  humerus, radius, ulna, hand, sacrum and coccyx, hip bone, femur, patella,
  tibia, fibula and foot.
- **Explore** tab: pick a bone from the list (or click it on the skeleton) to
  highlight it and read its Latin name, region, count, description and a fun
  fact.
- **Quiz** tab: a bone is highlighted, find its name among four choices.
